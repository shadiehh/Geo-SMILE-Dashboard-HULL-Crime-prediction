# Feature intervention cardinality

Feature model-based attribution faithfulness has been recomputed at **k = 10**, matching the Feature expert-reference top-10 set.

- Feature attribution accuracy: top 10 of 30
- Feature faithfulness intervention: masks the 10 highest-ranked predictors
- Geo attribution accuracy and faithfulness: both **k = 20**

The Feature columns are therefore evaluated at a common cardinality.
